# RG-337: published pool excavation allowance review

Reviewed 18 September 2026. This is source evidence for the future builder-approved setting in [RG-346](https://linear.app/royalglass/issue/RG-346), **not an approved PoolReady allowance or an excavation instruction**. The published numbers below are tied to particular pool constructions or contractors. They do not close the RG-346 builder-confirmation gate or authorise a numeric high-volume estimate.

The Firth numbers are the clearest published NZ example, but applying them to every residential pool would cross from a **system-specific specification** to an unsupported default. Digginit's fibreglass range is contractor practice, with an unresolved measurement convention. The NZ shell manufacturers instead point to a model's dig sheet and site/engineering specification.

## Code and safety boundary

- [MBIE, Building Code B1 — Structure](https://www.building.govt.nz/building-code-compliance/b-stability/b1-structure) covers loads on sitework and structural stability. The B1 page is not a pool excavation quantity schedule and gives neither universal side clearance nor base allowance.
- [WorkSafe, _Excavation safety_](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/topic-and-industry/excavation/excavation-safety-gpg/) calls for site-specific excavation design, attention to soil, slope, groundwater, services, nearby structures, safe access and ground-collapse controls. Its instruction to keep plant, vehicles, spoil and heavy loads outside the excavation's zone of influence **plus 1 m** unless specifically designed concerns surcharge at the **surface edge**. It is not 1 m of clearance around the pool shell or an extra metre of excavated width.
- WorkSafe states that this guide has not been updated to reflect the current Health and Safety at Work Act 2015 and regulations; it remains practical guidance rather than a current-law compliance determination.
- Pool-barrier dimensions in [MBIE F9 guidance](https://www.building.govt.nz/building-code-compliance/f-safety-of-users/pool-safety) are for restricting access to water. They do not define excavation width. The existing PoolReady **1 m construction placement allowance** is likewise a map-screening envelope, not a measured excavation footprint.

## What a later builder-approved setting must resolve

For each supported pool construction or model, record the source drawing/specification and version; whether the input dimensions are **water interior, shell exterior or outside masonry wall**; the side allowance's reference edge and whether it applies to each side; the base-layer and structural-floor thicknesses and reference level; floor profile and slope; drainage, bond beam, formwork and service-trench treatment; ground/retaining exclusions; and who approved use of the values and when. Preserve the applied version with each estimate. These measurements are candidates for review, not prefilled setting values.
