How does it work with Royal Glass?

At Royal Glass, we believe in making your project experience seamless and satisfying. With us, you can expect a smooth and efficient process from start to finish, delivering exceptional glass solutions for your home or business.

1. Initial Consultation

Whether it's through email, SMS, call, or social media, getting in touch with us is the first step. Our responsive team is ready to listen to your needs and answer any questions you may have.

2. Quotation

We'll discuss your requirements, preferences, and budget to provide you with a detailed and competitive quotation. This includes a breakdown of costs and an estimated timeline for your project.

3. Concept Design

Our technicians will create a concept design to bring your vision to life. We'll work closely with you to ensure the design meets your expectations and fits seamlessly into your space.

4. Site Measurement

Our technicians will visit your site to take precise measurements. This ensures that our products are tailored to fit perfectly, avoiding any surprises during installation.

5. Installation

Our installation team will handle the installation process with care and precision. We'll coordinate with you to schedule the installation at a convenient time and ensure everything is installed to the highest standards.

6. Feedback

After the installation, we'll follow up to ensure you're completely satisfied with our work. Your feedback is invaluable to us as we strive to continuously improve our services.
